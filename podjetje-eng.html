Overview

An adaptable, efficient team, always looking for new challenges

Combined, we have more than 20 years of experience. The company was established in 2010 with the vision to technically support the industry while developing it through innovation.

We focus on renewing worn out tools used in the processes of the textile industry and its close derivatives, using our own patented procedure. With innovative methods we upgrade and improve manufacturing processes. We are also the regional representative and distributor for leading South Korean companies that support the textile industry and related sectors in South Korea, the Asian region and globally.
