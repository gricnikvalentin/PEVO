About us

Knowledge that renews industry

The company was established in 2010 with the vision to technically support the industry while developing it through innovation. We bring together local experts with more than 20 years of combined experience and knowledge in producing, treating and renewing ceramic and steel products.

We collaborate with the University of Ljubljana and are globally connected with leading South Korean manufacturers in the textile industry, as well as the Korea Textile Development Institute (KTDI). We have also developed and patented the process of renewing used products made of technical ceramics.
